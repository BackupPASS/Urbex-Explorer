A location appearing on PlingifyPlug Explorer does not mean that you have permission to enter it. You must not enter private property, restricted areas, buildings, rooftops or other locations without the appropriate permission and legal authority to do so.

PlingifyPlug does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, safety, current status, ownership, accessibility or legal accessibility of any location or information displayed.
